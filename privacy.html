Gym Planner

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Gym Planner ("the App") helps you plan workout routines, record exercises, and review your training history. We respect your privacy and aim to keep your workout information under your control.

1. Information you enter

The App lets you enter workout routines, exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, and workout history. Based on the App's current offline design, this information is stored locally on your device and is not sent to our servers.

2. Accounts and personal information

The App does not require you to create an account. We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, or other account registration details to use its core features.

3. Internet access and data sharing

The App's core workout planning and tracking features work without an internet connection. We do not sell your workout data or share it with advertisers. This policy assumes the App does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, crash-reporting services, or cloud synchronization; if any are enabled, this section must be updated to describe them.

4. Data storage and deletion

Your workout data is kept on your device. You can manage or remove workout entries using the App's available controls. Uninstalling the App generally removes its locally stored data, subject to device backup and restore behavior. If you use device backups, copies may remain in backups managed by your device or backup provider.

5. Device permissions

The App does not need access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location for its core workout planning and tracking features. If future features request additional permissions, we will explain their purpose when requested and update this policy as needed.

6. Children's privacy

The App is intended for general workout planning and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the current App does not require accounts or transmit workout records to our servers, we do not knowingly collect children's personal information through its core features.

7. Security

Keeping workout data locally limits the need to transfer it over the internet. However, no storage method is completely secure. We recommend protecting your device with a passcode and keeping its operating system updated.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App's features or privacy practices change. The updated version will be posted here with a revised effective date.
